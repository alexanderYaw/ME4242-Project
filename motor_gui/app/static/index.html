Joystick

on-screen stick (no gamepad) released
Jog (default): push to keep the target moving that way, faster the further you push; let go and it stays put, the next push continues from there. Position: the stick points at a spot (full deflection = edge of reach). On-screen: drag the stick with the mouse or a finger; let go to hold. Gamepad: left stick steers, hold RT / R2 (or RB / R1) to move, B / Circle = torque off all (press any gamepad button first so the browser sees it). Moves follow the same pair rules and current limits.

tip_feed.py finds an orange rod by itself (or the colour you pick) and measures it with the depth camera. Mark rod ends works for any colour (e.g. the grey prototype): click both ends in the camera view; it stays until you press Forget. Moves to a target then follow a path that keeps the whole arm (constant-curvature model) clear of the rod, curling it over the rod when that is the only way round, and the joystick slides along the rod instead of pushing into it. Clearance is from the arm's centreline to the rod's surface: allow for the disc radius plus the model error (±10 mm).

Needs a fitted model (fit_tip_model.py) and, for closed loop, the camera feed (~/emio-venv/bin/python tip_feed.py). Moves follow the pair rules (one cable of each opposing pair pulls at a time). Stop / Esc / TORQUE OFF ALL interrupt a move.

Camera (live) — tracked marker outlined, rod in magenta / red
Camera view off: run ~/emio-venv/bin/python tip_feed.py

Torque must be on before a motor will move. Turning torque on holds the current position. Commands are clamped to the soft limits and the motor's firmware limits. Angles use the emioapi convention (pulse 2048 = 0°) until you calibrate; after that, angle, goal, slider and jog are relative to the calibrated 0 (stored in data/settings.json; the motor itself is not changed). "Absolute" is the emioapi angle. Cable displacement = (angle − zero) × drum radius. Esc = torque off all.
