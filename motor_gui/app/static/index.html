How the joystick works

Jog (default): push to keep the target moving that way, faster the further you push; let go and it stays put, the next push continues from there. Position: the stick points at a spot (full deflection = edge of reach). On-screen: drag the stick with the mouse or a finger; let go to hold. Gamepad: left stick steers, hold RT / R2 (or RB / R1) to move, B / Circle = torque off all (press any gamepad button first so the browser sees it). Moves follow the same pair rules and current limits. Invert x / y in Setup.

Obstacles

tip_feed.py finds orange and red rods (or a picked colour) and objects, and measures them with the depth camera. Only a marked rod is avoided.

From the arm's centreline to the rod's surface: allow for the disc radius plus the model error (±10 mm).

How obstacle avoidance works

tip_feed.py finds the orange and red rods by itself (or the colour you pick), and other objects of those colours or green (a tray, a piece: shape, size and the surface the camera sees; touching pieces are split; a tray's shaped pockets are found from depth), and measures them with the depth camera; they are shown in the views but not avoided. Mark rod ends works for any colour (e.g. the grey prototype): click both ends in the camera view; it stays until you press Forget, and only this rod is avoided. Moves to a target then follow a path that keeps the whole arm (constant-curvature model) clear of the rod, curling it over the rod when that is the only way round, and the joystick slides along the rod instead of pushing into it. The clearance is a model estimate, not a guarantee.

Reference

Conventions and commands for engineers.

Torque. Must be on before a motor moves; turning it on holds the current position. TORQUE OFF ALL (Esc, or B / Circle on a gamepad) makes every motor go limp; nothing holds the arm.

Stops. Hold all stops tip control and freezes powered motors where they are. Stop tip ends a tip move; Stop joystick ends joystick control; both hold.

Limits. Commands are clamped to the soft limits and the motor's firmware limits. Tip moves follow the pair rules (0/2 and 1/3 oppose; only one cable of each pair pulls), back off above 1000 mA and release everything above 1100 mA.

Angles. emioapi convention (pulse 2048 = 0°) until calibrated; after that angle, goal, slider and jog are relative to the calibrated 0. "Absolute" is the emioapi angle. Cable displacement = (angle − zero) × drum radius.
