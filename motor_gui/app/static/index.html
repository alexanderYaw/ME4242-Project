Needs a fitted model (fit_tip_model.py) and, for closed loop, the camera feed (~/emio-venv/bin/python tip_feed.py). Moves follow the pair rules (one cable of each opposing pair pulls at a time). Stop / Esc / TORQUE OFF ALL interrupt a move.

Torque must be on before a motor will move. Turning torque on holds the current position. Commands are clamped to the soft limits and the motor's firmware limits. Angles use the emioapi convention (pulse 2048 = 0°) until you calibrate; after that, angle, goal, slider and jog are relative to the calibrated 0 (stored in data/settings.json; the motor itself is not changed). "Absolute" is the emioapi angle. Cable displacement = (angle − zero) × drum radius. Esc = torque off all.
