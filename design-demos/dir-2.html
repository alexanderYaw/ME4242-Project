Engineering notes

  • Angles follow emioapi: angle = (2048 − pulse) × 2π / 4096 until calibrated; then relative to the calibrated 0.
  • Cable displacement = (angle − zero) × drum radius (sign inverted if set).
  • Goals are clamped to the soft limits and the motor's firmware limits.
  • Moves follow the pair rules: 0/2 and 1/3 oppose; only one of each pair pulls; a cable is released before its opposite pulls. Above 1000 mA a pulling motor backs off; above 1100 mA everything is released (backend limits).
  • Close Emio Labs / SOFA first: a running scene overrides the motors.
