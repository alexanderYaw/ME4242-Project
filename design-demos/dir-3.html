Diagnostics

LevelSourceConditionRaw
  • Angles follow emioapi: angle = (2048 − pulse) × 2π / 4096 (pulse 2048 = 0°). After calibration, angle, goal, slider and jog are relative to the calibrated zero; soft limits stay absolute.
  • Cable displacement = (angle − zero) × drum radius. The zero is stored in data/settings.json; the motor itself is not changed.
  • Turning torque on holds the current position. Commands are clamped to the soft limits and the motor's firmware limits.
  • Tip moves follow the pair rules (0/2 and 1/3 oppose; only one of each pair pulls) and back off above the backend's current limit.
  • Camera feed: ~/emio-venv/bin/python tip_feed.py. Tip model: fit_tip_model.py data/tip_<time>.csv. GUI: cd motor_gui && ./run.sh.
  • This prototype is simulated: commands go to the in-page simulator (see the SIMULATION strip's command log).
